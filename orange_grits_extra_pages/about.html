About the Orange Grits Café

Founded by Robert Ivey — Orange Grits Café is more than a shop. It’s a cross-cultural food metaverse born from the Eastside of Cleveland, made in partnership with real people, real flavor, and real communities.

Products like Sneaky Sweet Heet, ZMeta Energized Chocolate, and Little Robert’s Orange Grits are all part of a global flavor journey, powered by the people.

We are proud to partner with places like Loiter EC, Nubian Markets, and the Chocolate Rebellion collective. From food to futures, flavor is our resistance.
